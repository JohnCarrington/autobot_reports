4. ISOLATION REPLAY — DOES NOT FIRE
------------------------------------------------------------------------
The nms_v3_classifier.py module documents itself as 'pure' and 'zero
I/O'. In practice, when the state machine is run in isolation from a
fresh state against the frozen candle corpus, it produces NO
transitions, even on days where production V3 is documented in
`reports-public/nms_v3_replacement_20261013/report.md §3.4` to have
fired STRONG_DOWN or STRONG_UP.

Verified on 2026-10-09 (report says STRONG_DOWN at 12:35 UTC, 335 min
after London Open): isolated replay yields 0 transitions, final state
RANGE, scores up/down = None.  (Both pre- and post-BST-patch to the
hardcoded 2026-only BST window.)

Reason: production V3 relies on pre-London state bridges that supply
the state machine with:
  - confirmed_swings seeded from fractal-pivot detection with lookback
  - EMAs(7/13/21) primed from pre-London bars
  - persisted session anchors (london_open_price, OR window)
  - evidence memory (E1 OR breakout, E3 continuation, E4 confirmed HL)

Rebuilding those bridges standalone is a non-trivial engineering task
(outside the research-only scope). This confirms the research team's
own test harness note at report.md §3.4: '175 min (test harness seeds
anchor)'.

5. PRE-REPAIR LIVE RECORDS ARE UNRELIABLE
------------------------------------------------------------------------
Per the user brief: 'NMS V3 was repaired on 13 October 2026. Pre-repair
live execution records must not be treated as reliable evidence of
correctly functioning NMS.'

Even if we had a time-series log of pre-repair V3 states (we don't),
those states would be unreliable.

6. CONCLUSION: WHAT WE CAN AND CANNOT DO
------------------------------------------------------------------------
CAN:
 - Catalogue the 7 named days in the research team's §3.4 replay as
   the only genuine V3 timing data available.
 - Record one post-repair snapshot (today's state).
 - Compute the research team's aggregate claim: 20/23 correct
   direction, 3 wrong, mean delay 158 min — this is the authoritative
   V3-vs-truth number, as published in the report.

CANNOT:
 - Correlate V3 against the full 277-movement census of this study.
 - Reconstruct V3 states for 2024 and 2025 — classifier doesn't fire
   in isolation; no stored time series.
 - Produce TP30/SL12 or TP40/SL12 trade results for individual
   genuine V3 fires beyond the 7 named days in the §3.4 table.

What IS reportable is the research team's own result: V3 fires with
~87 % direction accuracy on the 23 directional days they tested, with
a mean delay of 158 min after London Open. 158 min after a 09:00 UK
open (BST) places the earliest fire time at ~11:30 UK — by which
point, from our own Phase-1 corpus, 65 % of trend-day fires are
already > 50 % of the way through the move.

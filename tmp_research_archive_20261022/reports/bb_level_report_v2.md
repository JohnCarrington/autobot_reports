Detection rules (v2):
- TOUCH: one-sided reach-or-pierce with 3p tolerance (R-side: `high >= level - 3`; S-side: `low <= level + 3`).
- SIGNED PIERCE DEPTH: R-side = `high - level`; S-side = `level - low` (positive = through level, negative = kiss).
- BREAKOUT CAP: events with peak signed pierce > 20p are excluded (treated as breakouts, not interactions).
- EVENT DEDUP: consecutive TOUCHING bars fold into one event; anchor is the first touching bar; the event ends on the first bar that no longer touches (bar range clears the 3p touch band).
- COINCIDENCE: band edge within 3p of the level at the ANCHOR bar only. LEVEL-only cohort = anchor gap in the 3–10p grey zone OR ≥ 10p; BAND-only = bar extreme within 3p of moving band AND every one of the seven pivots ≥ 10p from that band edge at anchor.
- FORWARD WINDOW: 12 bars starting from the bar IMMEDIATELY AFTER the event's last touching bar. Turn thresholds count only if reached BEFORE any close beyond the level.
- Cohorts OUTER (R1..R3 / S1..S3) and P are ALWAYS reported separately, never pooled. BAND-only counts are reported under each cohort as its control set (identical numbers by construction).

from Pass 4/5: BIG25 (>= +25p), W (0 < r < +25p), L (r <= 0).

**Conventions.**
- 5-min bars, ×10000 pip units, bar-open timestamps UTC.
- Entry bar = last bar whose open <= `timestamp_open`.
- Lifetime bars = entry bar through last bar whose open <= `timestamp_close`, inclusive.
- t7 = first lifetime bar whose favourable extreme reaches +7.0p from `entry`
  (BUY: `high - entry`; SELL: `entry - low`). If none, `NO_T7`.
- BB(20,2): SMA20 and population sigma over the 20 closes ending at the bar.
  `band_width_pips` = 4·sigma.
- ATR14 (Wilder): TRs from bar 1 to bar N (need prior close); seed = mean(TR[1..14]);
  update ATR = (ATR·13 + TR)/14. Uses >=40 prior bars where available; where an entry
  falls within the first 40 bars of the loaded window, flagged in the Flags section.
- Prior day's CSV is loaded (up to 5 prior calendar days) to seed ATR/BB continuity.
- Lifetime MFE = max favourable extreme across all lifetime bars, recomputed
  from candles. Log's `mfe_pips` is null for 59/157 fires (pre-2026-06-23),
  so we compute uniformly from bars. Cross-check on the 99 fires where log MFE
  is populated is included in Flags.
- `runner_room` = lifetime MFE >= 12.0p, i.e. price ran >=5p past the +7 trigger.

## CAVEATS (honestly)

1. **The recorded stream predates §24.** The `qm_candidates.jsonl`
   entries were written before tonight's `_transition()` was rebuilt
   to stamp confidence and to include the §24 branch. Every
   `LEVEL_ACCEPTED` you see in the recorded stream is a *terminal*
   under the old machine. The re-walk above exists precisely to
   redo those moments — we take the recorded LA bar as ground truth
   for the classifier's call and apply §24's reclaim rule to the
   actual 5m closes that followed. That is the closest read-only
   test possible short of resimulating the classifier.

2. **Weights are pre-recalibration.** `_REJECTION_WEIGHTS` and the
   memory weights (`mem_first_touch`, `mem_test_number_3plus`,
   `mem_role_reversal_retest`, `mem_recency_fresh`) are the current
   operator defaults, provisional until the tape-driven recalibration
   ships. Today's BOUNCE_A hypothetical score of 7 sits exactly at
   the floor — a recalibration that shifted `mem_role_reversal_retest`
   from +4 to +3 would drop it to 6 (band `possible`, refused). A
   recalibration that raised `close_inside_bb` from +2 to +3 would
   push it to 8 either way. Interpret the score-vs-floor margin
   with those uncertainty bounds.

3. **Memory factor lookup is best-effort by cohort.** The re-walk
   fetches memory records by `(symbol, level_type)` and picks the
   most recent update ≤ the query timestamp. `level_type` was
   inferred from the zone's dominant member (BB_U for the 13548.53
   cluster). For BOUNCE_C's 13532.267 zone the memory returned
   empty — meaning either the BB_L cohort had no update ≤ 13:15Z
   today, or the zone's dominant type was not matched. This is a
   read-side approximation; the production wiring would receive the
   `level_type` directly from the mapper output attached to the
   candidate.

4. **No 5m EURUSD reversals today ≥25p.** The scan was symmetric
   (GBPUSD + EURUSD, both directions, 25p threshold). EURUSD ranged
   1.15900-1.16330 all session; no leg cleared the threshold. All

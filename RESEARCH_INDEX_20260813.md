## 5. Infrastructure fixes

1. **D1 cache corruption** — commits **fe92ecb** (2026-08-13T14:32Z) + **2e294bd** (2026-08-13T14:42Z).
   - **Broken:** `htf_cache.normalize_ig_hist_to_candles` applied a hard `- bucket_secs` shift to every REST DAY bar's `bucket_epoch`, dating from when IG's DAY snapshotTime was end-of-window. IG's current DAY snapshotTime is the trading day expressed as a naïve Europe/London date; after `tz_localize` + shift, weekday bars were landing on weekend `bucket_epoch` labels and colliding with legitimate weekend bars. Startup gap-fill merged four such bars into `cache/htf/GBPUSD_D1.json`; the on-disk cache carried weekend-labelled bars with weekday OHLC.
   - **For how long:** at least back through the last 25 fire-dates in the cache, i.e. late June onward; every fire-date in that window disagreed with a 5m-resampled reconstruction by 5–316 pips per level, only 2026-08-07 was within 0.01p (report 13, Section 4).
   - **Fix:** added `_drop_weekend_labelled_d1` write-side guard in `htf_cache.save_candles_to_cache`; rebuilt `cache/htf/GBPUSD_D1.json` from the 5m archive with a 22:00-UTC (FX day) boundary (141 bars); verified against a live IG DEMO REST fetch that the Wed 08-12 REST bar's OHLC (H 13544.7 / L 13484.1 / C 13496.2) matches the broker's verified daily; verified the corrected snapshotTime convention and removed the stale `- bucket_secs` shift for both H1 and D1.

2. **H1 one-hour rotation** — same commit set (`2e294bd`).
   - **Broken:** the same `- bucket_secs` shift was applied to H1 REST fills, rotating each H1 bar backward by one hour. `cache/htf/GBPUSD_H1.json` accordingly held bars whose OHLC belonged to the hour after their timestamp.
   - **For how long:** since the shift was introduced (predates this window). Only visible on the H1 cache; pierce reports 11 + 12 synthesised H1 from the 5m archive by resample so were not affected, confirmed in report 14.
   - **Fix:** same commit — corrected bucket_epoch derivation removes the H1 rotation.

3. **News-release dedup scope** — commit **9cb6efc** (2026-08-12T13:09Z).
   - **Broken:** the shared release-dedup ledger keyed on `(event_name, currency, release_minute)` with no `symbol` and no `module`. First ARM by any (symbol, module) marked the ledger and suppressed every other (symbol, module) for the same release for `NEWS_RELEASE_DEDUP_HOURS`.
   - **For how long:** observed collateral suppressions on 2026-08-07 NFP (EURUSD claimed the identity, GBPUSD logged 1,206 consecutive suppressions) and 2026-08-12 CPI (GBPUSD "ARM SUPPRESSED (dedup)" age=591s at 12:34). Extent backward unaudited.
   - **Fix:** widened the key to `(event, ccy, release_minute, symbol, module)`. Both call sites (`news_strategy.py` IDLE→ARMED, `news_strategy_release_anchored.py` anchor start) now pass their symbol + module tag. Cross-symbol / cross-module ARMs are no longer collateral.

## 6. Open questions

1. **LEVEL_BOUNCE exit rule.** Report 17 shows an unmanaged 100p SL / EOD hold pays +2.5–3.3p mean at NEAR≥5, but 65% stop out and the mean is carried by the ~10% tail. Report 19 shows daily concurrency reaches 12 (worst-case 1200p exposure) with 66% same-direction clustering. The ladder telemetry added in commit 157d134 is writing `logs/level_bounce_ladder.jsonl` with per-touch pivot events + MFE/MAE peak. The exit rule (whether to trail, scale at a specific pivot cascade depth, or leave unmanaged) is to be derived from the live ladder telemetry once enough closes have accumulated.

2. **BB_H1_REJECT_ARM promotion.** The precondition (commit 21fb77d, report 8) is coded but flag OFF. The pass was replayed against corrupt pivots; direction of the effect is preserved by report 14's proximity-neutral finding but the specific replay numbers (armed vs blocked WR, runner concentration) should be regenerated against corrected pivots before the flag is promoted.

3. **BB_BOUNCE pivot-proximity re-audit.** Every BB_BOUNCE pivot-arithmetic finding on 08-13 (reports 3, 7, 9, 10) needs re-running against the corrected D1 cache. The outer-vs-inner effect (report 3), the near-miss cohort (report 7), and the approach-cluster taxonomy (reports 9, 10) may or may not survive; report 14 already found that the pivot-proximity axis flipped from "worse at level" to "no signal" on the pierce corpus.

4. **PIVOT_BREAK enablement risk.** `PIVOT_BREAK_ENABLED=1` is live; the derivation and sensitivity work in reports 1 + 2 ran against corrupt pivots. Whether the live coil/confirmation predicate still fires on the same 2–4 days the sweep found is unverified post-fix.

5. **AUTOK_EXCLUDE env drift.** Running-process `AUTOK_EXCLUDE=NEWS_CONTINUATION` but current code default is `NEWS_CONTINUATION,PIVOT_BREAK,H1_PIERCE,LEVEL_BOUNCE`. The env has not been updated to match the code additions; PIVOT_BREAK, H1_PIERCE, LEVEL_BOUNCE positions may currently be inside auto_k's scope.
